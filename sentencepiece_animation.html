🔷 SentencePiece Tokenization

Language-Agnostic Subword Tokenization

How SentencePiece Works: Unlike BPE and WordPiece, SentencePiece treats the input text as a raw character sequence INCLUDING SPACES (represented as ▁). No pre-tokenization needed!

🔄 Key Differences from BPE & WordPiece:

  • Space Treatment: Spaces are treated as regular characters (▁ symbol). No word boundaries needed!
  • No Pre-tokenization: Works directly on raw text without splitting words first
  • Language Agnostic: Perfect for languages without clear word boundaries (Chinese, Japanese, etc.)
  • Reversible: Can always reconstruct original text perfectly including spaces
  • Two Algorithms: Can use BPE or Unigram LM internally (we'll show BPE variant here)
  • Usage: Used by T5, ALBERT, XLNet, and many multilingual models
💡 Space Notation: In SentencePiece, spaces are encoded as ▁ (U+2581 "Lower One Eighth Block"). For example: "hello world" → "▁hello▁world"

Original Text → Final Tokens:
📌 "hello" → [▁he, ll, o]
📌 "hell" → [▁he, ll]
📌 "held" → [▁he, l, d]
